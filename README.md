# UTCompass 🧭
> *Navigate UTM Johor Bahru with precision.*

UTCompass is an integrated campus mobility and wayfinding application designed specifically for students, staff, and visitors at **Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM JB)**. It bridges daily transit gaps by consolidating real-time shuttle tracking, indoor/outdoor building navigation, parking occupancy, and feedback management into a single platform.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
Navigating the extensive UTM JB campus poses daily challenges for students and visitors:
* **Unpredictable Bus Transit:** Unclear schedules, long wait times, and a lack of live updates on bus locations.
* **Complex Campus Layout:** Difficulty finding specific lecture halls, laboratories, or faculty blocks using standard map software like Google Maps.
* **Parking Friction:** Wasted time searching for open parking spaces near faculties during peak hours.
* **Unheard Feedback:** Lack of a streamlined channel to report transit delays, broken amenities, or campus mobility issues.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 🚌 **Live GPS Bus Tracking & Routes:** Real-time location tracking of campus shuttles, live route maps, and accurate arrival time estimates.
* 📍 **Interactive Building & Lab Directory:** Search tool pinpointing exact building codes, block numbers, floors, and specific labs (e.g., *Makmal Biologi T02*) with direct walking routes.
* 🅿️ **Real-Time Parking Status:** Live visibility into available parking slots near major faculties and residential colleges.
* ⚠️ **Issue & Delay Reporting System:** Dedicated reporting module allowing students to submit feedback and log transit delays directly to management.
* 🔔 **Smart Push Notifications:** Timely alerts regarding schedule changes, route detours, or campus mobility emergencies.

---

## 👥 Target Audience & Stakeholders
* **Primary Users:** UTM JB Undergraduate & Postgraduate Students, Foundation/UTMSpace Students.
* **Secondary Users:** UTM Faculty members, campus visitors, and university administration.
